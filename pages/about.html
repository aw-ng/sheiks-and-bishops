About Us

For years, Sheiks & Bishops has partnered with the Federal Government of Nigeria to deliver secure, scalable, and reliable IT security solutions. We believe that fighting crime requires strong digital security, and we are committed to providing the best.


Our History

Sheiks & Bishops is an Information Technology Consultancy Company formed in 1994 to provide proactive cutting edge technological service in matters of security aimed at making security consciousness a culture.

The Company has its Head office at 8 Iro Dan Musa Street, Asokoro, Abuja, with branch offices at 10 Mokuolu street, Anthony Village, Lagos & Ministry of Police Affairs, 11th Floor, Federal Secretariat, Abuja.

We have a reputation in the Security and IT Industry for designing and developing practical, state-of-the-art Software and Hardware that are quick to implement, and easy to use.

 

Our Vision

To be an IT Consulting firm that provides proactive cutting-edge technological services in matters of security (be it personal, vehicular or corporate) aimed at making security consciousness a culture.


Our Mission

To deliver innovative IT and cybersecurity solutions that protect organizations, strengthens digital resilience, and empowers sustainable business growth through trusted technology.

We are committed to providing quality and satisfactory service to our esteemed customers/clients by adding outstanding value to our products. Our service approach offers comprehensive client support in both hardware and software. Continuous Research and Development set off our dedication and efforts.
