About us

Making security consciousness a culture

Sheiks & Bishop is an Information Technology consultancy formed in 1994 to provide proactive, cutting-edge technological services in matters of security — aimed at making security consciousness a culture.

Our head office is at 94 Yakubu Gowon Crescent, Asokoro, Abuja, with branch offices at 10 Mokuolu Street, Anthony Village, Lagos, and the Ministry of Police Affairs, 11th Floor, Federal Secretariat, Abuja.

We have a team of professionals with several years of experience, functioning as a group of experts operating from a focal point. In association with other firms and external experts, a whole range of projects become easily realized.

We hold a reputation in the security and IT industry for designing and developing practical, state-of-the-art software and hardware that are quick to implement and easy to use.

We continue to focus on the expansion of our operations and to take advantage of significant growth opportunities, leveraging our core strength to bring even greater value to our customers. We are committed to providing quality, satisfactory service to our esteemed customers and clients by adding outstanding value to our products — our service approach offers comprehensive client support in both hardware and software, and continuous research and development sets off our dedication and efforts.

Our vision

To be an IT consulting firm that provides proactive, cutting-edge technological services in matters of security — be it personal, vehicular, or corporate — aimed at making security consciousness a culture.
